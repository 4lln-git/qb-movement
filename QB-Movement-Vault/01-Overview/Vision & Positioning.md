# Vision & Positioning

## Where you are today
- Current site: **[theqbmovement.ca](https://www.theqbmovement.ca)** — built on **Weebly**.
- Weebly is fine for a brochure site (info, contact, maybe basic booking), but it is a **dead end** for a product:
  - No real user accounts / roles
  - No subscription logic beyond basic
  - No app, no drawing canvas, no gamification, no data ownership
  - You can't ship a mobile app from it

## Where you want to go
A **product**, not a website. The reference quality bar is **[House of Athlete](https://houseofathlete.com)**:
- Clean, bold, athletic brand
- Programs & content gated behind membership
- Feels like a platform, not a flyer

…plus things House of Athlete does **not** have, which become your moat:
- ⭐ A **drawable digital playbook** (your screenshot reference)
- RPG-style [[QB Skill Tree]] progression
- AI [[Throw Analysis AI]]
- A unified [[Coach Dashboard]] that makes the business scalable

## Positioning statement
> **QB Movement is the daily operating system for quarterback development** — where players train, draw plays, and level up; parents get visibility; and coaches run their whole business in one place.

## Why this wins
1. **Daily habit, not one-off camps.** Subscriptions + streaks + challenges = recurring revenue and retention.
2. **Multi-sided.** Players pay, parents are sold, coaches scale, schools buy teams, recruiters discover. Each side reinforces the others.
3. **Defensible features.** The playbook + skill tree + AI combo doesn't exist together anywhere today (per your own research).

## Brand cues to borrow from House of Athlete
- Full-bleed athlete photography / video hero
- High contrast, limited palette, big bold type
- Membership framed as joining a *movement*, not buying a *subscription*
- Mobile-first everything

> [!tip] Brand asset to define early
> Lock the color palette, logo, and one hero font before design starts — it makes every screen downstream consistent. Put samples in `_attachments/`.
