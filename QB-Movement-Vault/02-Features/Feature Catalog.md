> [!info] Why this order
> Build the **daily habit loop first** (training content + journal + skill tree + playbook + gamification). That drives retention and word of mouth. Then the **business tools** (coach dashboard, teams) to scale revenue. Then **advanced/expansion** (AI, recruiting, marketplace, store) once you have an engaged base. Full detail in [[Roadmap & Phases]].

## Quick spec per feature

### 1. QB Skill Tree 🎮 → [[QB Skill Tree]]
Throw Power / Accuracy / Pocket Presence / Leadership / Football IQ as RPG bars. Level up, earn badges, unlock drills.

### 2. Throw Analysis (AI) → [[Throw Analysis AI]]
Player records 10 throws; AI analyzes mechanics; coach reviews after.

### 3. Parent Dashboard → [[Parent Dashboard]]
Attendance, hours trained, weekly report, coach feedback, next session, homework drills.

### 4. Digital Playbook ⭐ → [[Digital Playbook]]
Tap a coverage → draw the route → immediate feedback. The flagship.

### 5. Weekly Challenges
Every Monday a quest set (e.g. 500 throws, 5 ladder sessions, 3 mobility workouts, 1 film breakdown). Completion = XP. → [[Gamification]]

### 6. Film Room → [[Film Room]]
Upload game footage; coach comments **on the timeline** ("0:14 — feet too narrow → widen your base").

### 7. Leaderboards
Weekly: most throws, fastest improvement, most workouts, camp attendance, longest streak. → [[Gamification]]

### 8. Camps
Beyond registration: countdown, available spots, digital waiver, **QR check-in**, attendance, photos, coach notes, completion **certificate**.

### 9. Digital Combine
Standardized testing: 40-yard, vertical, broad jump, throw velocity, throw distance, reaction time. Updated yearly with progress charts.

### 10. QB Journal
Per practice: how'd today go, energy, confidence, arm soreness, goals, free-text entry. Builds long-term habits (and powers analytics).

### 11. Coach Dashboard → [[Coach Dashboard]]
Today's sessions, upcoming athletes, payments, messages, film reviews, attendance, progress, parent comms, analytics. **Where the business becomes scalable.**

### 12. Marketplace
Other specialists (speed, nutrition, mental, physio, strength, recruiting) offer services through the app. Revenue share.

### 13. Recruiting Hub
HS athlete profile: height, weight, school, GPA, highlight videos, combine metrics, coach references, camp history. Parents share with recruiters.

### 14. Equipment Store
In-app store: gloves, footballs, resistance bands, cones, ladders, gear. Members get discounts. (Likely Shopify-backed — see [[Integrations & Services]].)

### 15. Seasonal Programs
Summer QB / off-season strength / spring ball / in-season maintenance / winter mechanics. Unlock **automatically** based on the calendar.
